01 About me

Most businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. I build the systems that fix it.

I'm Maryam Zarafshan, a GoHighLevel specialist with 3+ years of hands-on experience and a BSCS from UMT Lahore. I don't just design pages — I engineer complete growth systems that hold up under real-world traffic.

My focus is workflow automation, AI agents, campaigns, lead capture and funnel building. I care about the details most people skip — duplicate checks, failure paths, maintainable tagging and reporting you can actually trust — because those are what make a system survive production.

Every build ships snapshot-ready, so agencies and businesses can clone it, scale it and spend their time selling instead of fire-fighting inside the CRM. I've delivered 60+ high-converting funnels across 15+ industries, from real estate and insurance to home services, education and healthcare.
